01 Supply model

Capacity, not a bundle.

We buy high-capacity transit from international carriers over multiple routes, manage it as one resilient network, and hand dedicated bandwidth to the operators who serve homes, businesses and communities.

  1. A

    Upstream providers

    International carriers, global networks, IXPs and regional fibre — multiple routes for redundancy.

  2. B

    Endwave

    Network access and bandwidth management. Redundant links, low latency, flexible contracts.

  3. C

    Wholesale customers

    ISPs, WISPs, fibre operators and carriers buy in bulk to resell or run their own networks.

  4. D

    Last-mile distribution

    Fibre, fixed wireless, 4G/5G and Wi-Fi across urban and rural coverage areas.

  5. E

    End users

    Businesses, schools, homes, hospitals, factories and rural communities — better connected.

08 Notes

Before you request.

01Who is wholesale capacity for?

Internet service providers and network operators who carry their own subscribers' traffic and need upstream capacity that behaves predictably — not a household broadband package.

02Is the capacity shared or throttled?

No. Capacity is dedicated to you and uncontended. You get the full committed rate at all hours.

03How is the capacity delivered?

On fibre, handed off at the tower site, PoP or partner data centre nearest your network — Raxio Uganda, ADC Kenya, iColo NBO1 & NBO2, or Raxio Tanzania. Your technician splices on site and we commission the link together.

04Which countries do you cover?

Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania. Share your location or site number and we'll confirm the nearest point we can serve you from.

05How is capacity priced?

Per Mbps per month, in bands. The rate steps down as your link gets bigger. Billing is available in local currency or USD.

06Can I add capacity later?

Yes. Upgrade the same link as your subscribers grow — your new band's rate applies from the next billing cycle.

07Do you offer IP addresses and BGP?

Yes. Endwave runs its own network as AS329846. We support static IP allocations and BGP sessions for operators with their own ASN. Talk to our engineering team for details.

08Do you offer colocation?

Yes. Colocation is available at our partner data centres — Raxio Uganda, ADC Kenya, iColo NBO1 and NBO2 in Kenya, and Raxio Tanzania — with capacity delivered straight to your rack.
