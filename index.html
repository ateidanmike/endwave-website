12 Notes

Before you request.

01Can I buy just one service?

Yes. Every service can be taken on its own. Combining them simply means one account, one invoice and systems that are already connected.

02Who is wholesale capacity for?

Internet service providers and network operators who carry their own subscribers' traffic and need upstream capacity that behaves predictably — from 100 Mbps to 100 Gbps.

03Is the capacity shared or throttled?

No. Capacity is dedicated to you and uncontended. You get the full committed rate at all hours.

04Do you offer BGP, IP addresses and colocation?

Yes. Endwave runs its own network as AS329846. We support static IP allocations and BGP sessions, and colocation is available at Raxio Uganda, ADC Kenya, iColo NBO1 & NBO2 and Raxio Tanzania.

05Can I keep my existing phone numbers?

In most cases, yes. We help port numbers to our VoIP platform and can issue new local numbers in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania.

06Do you set up the call centre equipment?

Yes. We scope the number of seats, configure IVR and queues, supply headsets and handsets where needed, and train your supervisors.

07How do I get a sender ID for bulk SMS?

We register your branded sender ID with the networks on your behalf. You can send from the web portal or connect your systems through our API.

08Does the billing system work with M-Pesa?

Yes. Payments from M-Pesa, Airtel Money and cards reconcile automatically against invoices, and overdue customers get SMS reminders.

09Which countries do you cover?

Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, for every service. Share your location and we'll confirm what we can deliver there.
