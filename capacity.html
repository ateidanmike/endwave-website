01 How capacity flows

From the world's carriers to your subscribers.

We buy high-capacity transit from international carriers over multiple routes, manage it as one resilient network and hand dedicated bandwidth to the operators who serve homes, businesses and communities.

A

Upstream providers

International carriers, global networks, IXPs and regional fibre, on multiple routes for redundancy.

B

Endwave

Network access and bandwidth management. Redundant links, low latency, flexible contracts.

C

Wholesale customers

ISPs, WISPs, fibre operators and carriers buy in bulk to resell or run their own networks.

D

Last-mile distribution

Fibre, fixed wireless, 4G/5G and Wi-Fi across urban and rural coverage areas.

E

End users

Businesses, schools, homes, hospitals, factories and rural communities, better connected.

02 How we deliver

Three ways to land capacity at your site.

We pick the link that suits your location, budget and timeline, and can combine two of them for a primary and a backup path.

01 · FIBRE

Fibre hand-off

Dedicated fibre to your tower, PoP or data centre for the highest capacity and lowest latency, up to 100 Gbps.

02 · STARLINK

07 Before activation

What your site needs.

Have these ready and we can usually commission the link on the day the site is confirmed.

Site readiness

Fibre, router & power

Last-mile fibre pulled to your BTS, a router with BGP or static routing and a free optical port (for example an SFP-enabled MikroTik), and power redundancy, strongly recommended.

On the day

Hardware & splicing

SFP modules, 1.25G 10 km (Huawei, Sopto or BIDI one-core), and a splicing technician on standby once the site is confirmed.
