How Endwave NextGen Worldwide collects, uses and protects personal data when you use this website or contact us.

This policy explains how Endwave NextGen Worldwide ("Endwave", "we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website and when you contact us. We process personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, as they apply to us.

Personal data processed in delivering our services to customers (for example, subscriber records in Endwave Billing, call recordings in the Call Centre or messages sent through Bulk SMS) is covered by the service agreement with that customer, not by this website policy.

1. Who we are

Endwave NextGen Worldwide provides wholesale internet capacity, VoIP and voice, call centres, bulk SMS, billing systems and e-commerce in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania. For the personal data described here, Endwave is the data controller.

2. Personal data we collect

Information you give us

  • Service and quote requests: your name, company, email address, mobile number, country, site location or tower number, the services and capacity you are interested in, and any notes you add.
  • Billing demo requests: your name, company or ISP name, email, phone, approximate subscriber numbers, router details, service type and current billing platform.
  • Emails, calls and WhatsApp messages you send us, and the contact details they come from.

Our website forms prepare a message in your own email app; nothing is sent until you choose to send it. We receive only what you send.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: when you load the site, our hosting provider and the font service we use receive your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, as part of delivering the site to you.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies on this website.

3. How we use personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry, assess feasibility and prepare a quotation.
  • To arrange demos, site surveys and service setup.
  • To enter into and manage a service agreement with you or your organisation.
  • To keep records of our communications and meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.

We will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal data on one or more of these grounds: your consent, for example when you send us a request; to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and to perform that contract; to comply with a legal obligation; and for our legitimate interests in running and securing our business, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

5. Who we share it with

  • Our teams in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, so the right country team can respond.
  • Service providers who help us operate, such as website hosting, email and communication providers, bound to protect your data and use it only on our instructions.
  • Regulators, courts or law-enforcement authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Transfers outside your country

Some of our providers, including website hosting and email services, may store or process data outside Kenya, Uganda or Tanzania. Where this happens we rely on safeguards permitted by the applicable law, such as the provider's contractual and security commitments.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and quotation records for as long as needed to respond and follow up, generally up to 24 months if no agreement follows. Records linked to a service agreement are kept for the life of the agreement and afterwards for as long as tax, accounting and regulatory rules require. We then delete or anonymise them.

8. Your rights

Subject to the law that applies to you, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • object to or restrict processing, including direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already done;
  • receive your data in a portable format where applicable.

To use these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the regulator in your country: the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya), the Personal Data Protection Office (Uganda) or the Personal Data Protection Commission (Tanzania).

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including restricted access, encrypted connections and secure systems. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information, such as passwords or payment card numbers, by email.

10. Cookies

This website does not set cookies for analytics or advertising. Your browser may store standard files, such as cached copies of pages and fonts, to load the site faster. Our customer platforms (Billing, Wholesale Operations and Call Centre) use cookies needed to keep you signed in; those are described in each platform.

11. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
