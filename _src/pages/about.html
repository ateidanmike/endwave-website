02 Why six services

Because operators were stitching it together themselves.

A typical network buys bandwidth from one supplier, voice from a carrier, SMS from a gateway and billing from a software vendor. Four contracts, four invoices and four support lines, none of which talk to each other.

Endwave puts all of it on one network. The capacity carries the calls, the billing system sends reminders through our SMS, and the e-commerce store settles through the same payment rails. One account manager, one invoice, one number to call.
